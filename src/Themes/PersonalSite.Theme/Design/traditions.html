Hungarian Traditions

The Bride's Dance

Guests queue to dance with the bride for a very short time, each placing money into a basket.

The Stealing of the Bride

Friends playfully kidnap the bride and the groom must complete tasks to win her back.

The Bouquet Toss

Whoever catches the bouquet is said to be next to marry.

Clinking Glasses and Tapping Plates

When guests clink and tap in unison, the newlyweds kiss.

Cutting the Cake

The couple cuts the wedding cake together as a symbol of shared life.
