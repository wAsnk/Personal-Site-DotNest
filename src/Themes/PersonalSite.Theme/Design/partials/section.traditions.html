Hungarian Traditions

A glimpse into the customs that make a Hungarian wedding truly unforgettable

The Bride's Dance

Guests queue to dance with the bride for a short moment while placing money into a basket as a joyful wish for prosperity.

The Stealing of the Bride

Friends playfully kidnap the bride and the groom must complete humorous tasks to win her back.

The Bouquet Toss

The bouquet is tossed to unmarried women and the one who catches it is said to be next to marry.

Clinking Glasses and Tapping Plates

When guests clink and tap in unison, the newlyweds kiss. The louder the crowd, the longer the kiss.

Cutting the Cake

The couple cuts the cake together as their first shared act as husband and wife.
