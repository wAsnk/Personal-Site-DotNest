Hungarian Traditions

A glimpse into the customs that make a Hungarian wedding truly unforgettable

The Bride's Dance

The bride's dance is a beloved tradition where guests queue to dance with the bride for a very short time, each placing money into a basket.

The Stealing of the Bride

During the celebration, friends playfully kidnap the bride. The groom must search for her, often completing humorous tasks to win her back.

The Bouquet Toss

The bride tosses her bouquet to the unmarried women. Whoever catches it is said to be the next to marry.

Clinking Glasses and Tapping Plates

When guests clink glasses or tap plates in unison, the newlyweds must kiss. The louder the clinking, the longer the kiss.

Cutting the Cake

The couple cuts the wedding cake together, hand over hand, symbolising their first shared act as husband and wife.
